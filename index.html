Before you ask

Straight answers.

Does it actually block apps, or can I cheat?
It really blocks. Sessions use Apple's Screen Time APIs to seal off the apps you choose for the full duration — no easy bypass. You decide what gets blocked before you start.
How is this different from Opal, Forest, or Freedom?
Those apps do one thing: block distractions. Kairos handles the before, during, and after — it reads your state, scores your focus live, learns your peak windows, and turns your progress into an identity you actually want to keep building. Blocking is just one piece.
Is it free?
Joining the waitlist is completely free. At launch there'll be a free tier to start your focus practice, plus affordable paid plans (a student plan included). Waitlist members lock in founding-member pricing that won't exist after public launch.
iPhone or Android?
Kairos launches on iPhone first. Join the waitlist with any email and we'll let you know the moment Android is ready too.
When does it launch?
We're in private beta right now. Early access opens to waitlist members first, in small batches by join order — not a public flood. The earlier you join, the sooner you get in.
