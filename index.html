The Problem

You feel it every day.

The gap between who you are and who you know you could be. It widens every time you reach for your phone.

🌊

Drowning in distraction

The average knowledge worker switches tasks every 3 minutes. Each switch costs 23 minutes of deep recovery time. Your best thinking never gets to happen.

📱

Built to steal your attention

Every app you use was designed by teams of engineers to consume your time. You're not weak. You're up against billion-dollar machines optimized for your dopamine.

📊

Busyness mistaken for progress

You end every day exhausted but unsatisfied. You moved fast, stayed busy, checked things off. But you never did the real work. The work that compounds.

⏳

The quiet erosion of potential

The most painful part? You know exactly what you're capable of. You just never quite get there. And the gap between potential and reality quietly grows.

Questions

The honest answers.

Kairos is a deep work operating system — a mobile and desktop app that helps you structure, track, and compound your focused attention over time. It combines session management, behavioral analytics, intelligent distraction blocking, and daily rituals into one coherent system. It's not a to-do list. It's not a timer. It's the layer of operating system your life was missing.
We're currently in final development. Founding members on the waitlist will receive early access before the public launch. We're targeting a launch in the coming months and will notify waitlist members at least 48 hours before opening to the general public.
Kairos will launch on iOS and macOS simultaneously, with Android and Windows following shortly after. The core experience is designed around mobile — where most distraction happens — with a rich desktop companion for session management and deep analytics.
Those tools solve parts of the problem. Kairos solves the whole thing. Forest gamifies surface-level focus. Freedom blocks sites. Notion tracks tasks. None of them understand your attention as an asset that compounds over time, give you a unified score of your focus quality, or build the behavioral intelligence to tell you exactly when and how you focus best. Kairos is the operating system — not a single tool.
Founding members get early access before public launch, a permanent discount on all Kairos plans (locked for life, not just the first year), direct input into the product roadmap, and a founding member badge in their profile. You're not just getting early access — you're becoming part of what Kairos is.
Absolutely. Your focus data is yours. We never sell it, share it, or use it to train models without your explicit consent. Your attention patterns are deeply personal — we treat them that way. Full privacy policy available at launch.
Joining the waitlist is completely free and commits you to nothing. When we launch, we'll offer a generous trial period so you can experience Kairos before committing to any plan. We're confident in what we're building — but we'd rather earn your trust than demand it.
